# Signal Analysis

Deep dive into how Oraculum's AI analyzes market signals.

## Signal Components

### Price Signal
Movement and momentum of market prices.

| Indicator | What It Measures |
|-----------|------------------|
| Trend | Direction of price movement |
| Momentum | Speed of price change |
| Volatility | Price fluctuation intensity |
| Support/Resistance | Key price levels |

### Volume Signal
Trading activity intensity and patterns.

| Indicator | What It Measures |
|-----------|------------------|
| Volume spike | Unusual trading activity |
| Volume trend | Increasing/decreasing interest |
| Volume profile | Distribution across prices |

### Flow Signal
Where capital is moving.

| Indicator | What It Measures |
|-----------|------------------|
| Net flow | Buy vs sell pressure |
| Whale activity | Large position changes |
| Retail flow | Small trader behavior |

### Sentiment Signal
Overall market mood.

| Indicator | What It Measures |
|-----------|------------------|
| Bullish % | Proportion betting YES |
| Conviction | Size of positions |
| Divergence | Price vs sentiment mismatch |

## Signal Strength

Signals are rated by strength:

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| ★★★★★ | Very strong signal |
| ★★★★☆ | Strong signal |
| ★★★☆☆ | Moderate signal |
| ★★☆☆☆ | Weak signal |
| ★☆☆☆☆ | Very weak signal |

## Confidence Score

Each signal includes a confidence percentage:

- **90%+** — High confidence
- **70-89%** — Moderate confidence
- **50-69%** — Low confidence
- **Below 50%** — Uncertain

## Using Signals

### Best Practices

1. **Don't rely solely on AI** — Use as one input among many
2. **Check signal age** — Fresh signals are more relevant
3. **Consider context** — External events affect accuracy
4. **Track performance** — Monitor which signals work for you

### Signal + Research

Combine AI signals with:
- Fundamental research
- News monitoring
- Cross-market analysis
- Expert opinions

## Limitations

The AI is not perfect. It cannot:
- Predict black swan events
- Account for unreleased information
- Guarantee profitable trades
- Replace human judgment

Use AI signals as a tool, not an oracle.
