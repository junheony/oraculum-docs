# AI Layer Overview

Oraculum's AI does not predict outcomes. It interprets markets.

## Philosophy

> "AI does not replace the market. AI helps the market speak clearly."

The AI layer serves as an intelligent filter that:
- Removes noise and manipulation
- Surfaces important patterns
- Highlights information asymmetries
- Detects early signals of change

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Noise Filtering

Markets are noisy. Our AI separates signal from noise:

- **Organic flow** vs **manipulation**
- **Informed trading** vs **noise trading**
- **Trend changes** vs **random fluctuation**

### 2. Pattern Recognition

Identifies recurring patterns across markets:

- Price formations
- Volume anomalies
- Cross-market correlations
- Timing patterns

### 3. Sentiment Analysis

Analyzes market sentiment through:

- Price momentum
- Trading velocity
- Position concentration
- Order flow imbalance

### 4. Anomaly Detection

Flags unusual activity that may indicate:

- Insider information
- Coordinated trading
- News leaks
- Market manipulation

## How It Works

```
Raw Market Data
      ↓
  Noise Filter
      ↓
Pattern Analysis
      ↓
Signal Extraction
      ↓
 Actionable Insights
```

## AI Signals

The AI produces various signal types:

| Signal | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Strong Buy | High conviction bullish signal |
| Buy | Moderate bullish signal |
| Neutral | No clear direction |
| Sell | Moderate bearish signal |
| Strong Sell | High conviction bearish signal |

## Accuracy Tracking

All AI signals are tracked for accuracy:

- Real-time performance metrics
- Historical accuracy rates
- Confidence calibration
- Signal decay analysis
