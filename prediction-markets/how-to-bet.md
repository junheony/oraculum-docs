# How to Bet

Learn how to participate in Oraculum prediction markets.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Connect Your Wallet

Open Oraculum through Telegram and connect your TON wallet.

### 2. Browse Markets

Explore available markets by category:
- Featured markets
- Trending markets
- Ending soon
- New markets

### 3. Research

Before betting, consider:
- Current market probability
- Recent price movements
- AI signal analysis
- External news sources

### 4. Place Your Bet

1. Select a market
2. Choose **YES** or **NO**
3. Enter your stake amount
4. Confirm transaction

### 5. Monitor Your Position

Track your active bets in your portfolio:
- Current value
- Profit/Loss
- Market movement

### 6. Resolution

When the event resolves:
- **Correct prediction** → Receive payout
- **Incorrect prediction** → Lose stake

## Betting Strategies

### Conservative
- Bet on high-probability outcomes
- Lower returns, lower risk
- Focus on markets near resolution

### Aggressive
- Seek undervalued opportunities
- Higher potential returns
- Requires more research

### Contrarian
- Bet against popular opinion
- High risk, high reward
- Requires conviction

## Tips for Success

1. **Start small** — Learn with smaller stakes
2. **Diversify** — Don't put all capital in one market
3. **Research** — Use AI signals and external sources
4. **Stay updated** — Markets move on new information
5. **Set limits** — Know when to exit

## Risk Warning

Prediction markets involve risk. Only bet what you can afford to lose. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
