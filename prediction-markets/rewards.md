# Rewards & Points

Earn rewards for participating in Oraculum prediction markets.

## Points System

Earn ORAC points through various activities:

| Activity | Points |
|----------|--------|
| Daily login | +10 |
| First prediction | +50 |
| Correct prediction | +100 |
| Win streak (3+) | Bonus multiplier |
| Referral | +500 |

## Earning Multipliers

### Prediction Streak
Consecutive correct predictions boost your rewards:

| Streak | Multiplier |
|--------|------------|
| 3 wins | 1.2x |
| 5 wins | 1.5x |
| 10 wins | 2.0x |
| 20 wins | 3.0x |

### Early Participation
Bet early in new markets for bonus points.

### High Conviction
Larger stakes earn proportionally more points.

## Leaderboard

### Leaderboard Rewards

## Achievements

Unlock special achievements:

| Achievement | Requirement |
|-------------|-------------|
| First Blood | First correct prediction |
| Streak Master | 10 consecutive wins |

## Referral Program

Invite friends and earn:

- **500 points** per successful referral
- **10%** of referee's earned points (first month)
- **Exclusive badges** for top referrers

### How to Refer

1. Get your unique referral code
2. Share with friends
3. They sign up using your code
4. Both earn bonus points
