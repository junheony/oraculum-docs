# Market Types

Oraculum offers various market types to suit different prediction scenarios.

## Binary Markets

The most common type — two possible outcomes.

**Example:**
> "Will BTC reach $100,000 by December 31, 2025?"

| Outcome | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| YES | BTC reaches $100,000 |
| NO | BTC does not reach $100,000 |

## Multi-Outcome Markets

Markets with more than two possible outcomes.

**Example:**
> "Who will win the 2024 Championship?"

| Outcome | Current Price |
|---------|---------------|
| Team A | 45% |
| Team B | 30% |
| Team C | 15% |
| Other | 10% |

## Range Markets

Predict where a value will fall within ranges.

**Example:**
> "What will be the ETH price on Jan 1, 2026?"

| Range | Current Price |
|-------|---------------|
| Below $2,000 | 10% |
| $2,000 - $3,000 | 25% |
| $3,000 - $5,000 | 40% |
| Above $5,000 | 25% |

## Parlay Bets

Combine multiple predictions for higher payouts.

**Example:**
> Bet on all three outcomes:
> 1. BTC above $80K ✓
> 2. ETH above $4K ✓
> 3. SOL above $200 ✓

All must be correct to win, but payout multiplies.

## Time-Based Markets

Markets that resolve at specific times.

- **Daily** — Resolves end of day
- **Weekly** — Resolves end of week
- **Monthly** — Resolves end of month
- **Event-based** — Resolves when event occurs

## Safe Bets

Lower-risk markets with higher probability outcomes.

- Typically 70%+ probability
- Lower returns but higher success rate
- Good for beginners
