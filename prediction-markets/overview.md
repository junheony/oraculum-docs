# Prediction Markets Overview

Oraculum's prediction markets allow you to bet on real-world outcomes and earn rewards for accurate predictions.

## What Are Prediction Markets?

Prediction markets are exchange-traded markets where participants trade contracts based on the outcomes of future events. The prices of these contracts reflect the probability of the events occurring.

## Market Categories

### Politics
- Elections and referendums
- Policy decisions
- Government actions

### Crypto
- Token price milestones
- Protocol upgrades and launches
- Regulatory decisions

### Technology
- Product releases
- AI developments
- Company milestones

### Sports
- Game outcomes
- Championship predictions
- Player achievements

### Economics
- Interest rate decisions
- Inflation data
- Employment figures

## How Markets Work

Each market has two possible outcomes:

| Position | Meaning | Payout |
|----------|---------|--------|
| **YES** | Event will occur | Win if correct |
| **NO** | Event will not occur | Win if correct |

### Price Interpretation

- Price of 70 = 70% probability
- Price of 30 = 30% probability
- Prices always sum to 100

## Getting Started

1. Browse available markets
2. Research the topic
3. Place your bet (YES or NO)
4. Wait for resolution
5. Collect winnings if correct
