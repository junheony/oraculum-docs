# How It Works

Oraculum transforms collective belief into actionable probability through a five-step process.

## The Process

### 01. Market Creation

A question is framed around a verifiable future event.

**Examples:**
- "Will BTC exceed $100,000 by Q1 2025?"
- "Will [Candidate] win the election?"
- "Will [Protocol] launch mainnet in 2025?"

**Requirements:**
- Clear, unambiguous outcome criteria
- Specific resolution date
- Verifiable through public sources

---

### 02. Capital Allocation

Participants express their views by allocating capital to YES or NO positions.

- **YES** — You believe the event will happen
- **NO** — You believe the event will not happen

The amount you stake reflects your conviction level.

---

### 03. Price Formation

As participants buy and sell positions, prices adjust in real-time.

```
Market Price = Collective Belief

70% YES price = 70% probability the event occurs
```

Prices between 0-100 represent the market's consensus probability.

---

### 04. AI Signal Extraction

Oraculum's AI layer continuously analyzes:

- **Flow patterns** — Where is capital moving?
- **Conviction levels** — How confident are participants?
- **Anomaly detection** — Unusual activity that may signal information

This creates high-fidelity signals from noisy market data.

---

### 05. Truth Emergence

When the event resolves:

- Correct predictions are rewarded
- Incorrect predictions lose their stake
- The market price becomes historical record of collective belief

**Truth is discovered, not declared.**

---

## Example Walkthrough

**Market:** "Will ETH 2.0 staking reach 50M ETH by end of 2025?"

1. Market opens at 50% (neutral)
2. Analysts publish bullish reports → Price rises to 65%
3. Network issues reported → Price drops to 45%
4. Major staking provider announces expansion → Price rises to 72%
5. End of 2025: 52M ETH staked → YES wins, holders paid out
