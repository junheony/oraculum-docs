# What is Oraculum?

Oraculum is a decentralized information market platform that harnesses collective intelligence to predict future events.

## Core Philosophy

> "The future cannot be known. But it can be priced."

Traditional prediction methods rely on experts, polls, or centralized authorities. Oraculum takes a different approach: **markets aggregate truth better than individuals.**

## How It's Different

### From Traditional Betting
- Not gambling — it's information discovery
- Markets reflect genuine belief, not odds set by bookmakers
- AI layer extracts meaningful signals from market movements

### From Expert Opinions
- No single point of failure
- Distributed knowledge from diverse participants
- Real capital at stake creates accountability

### From Polls & Surveys
- Real-time probability updates
- Incentive alignment through skin-in-the-game
- No sampling bias or response manipulation

## Use Cases

| Domain | Example Markets |
|--------|-----------------|
| Politics | Election outcomes, policy decisions |
| Crypto | Token prices, protocol upgrades |
| Technology | Product launches, AI breakthroughs |
| Sports | Game outcomes, player performance |
| Economics | Interest rates, inflation data |

## The Oracle Mechanism

Oraculum acts as a decentralized oracle — a bridge between real-world events and on-chain outcomes.

1. **Market Creation** — A question is framed around a verifiable event
2. **Price Discovery** — Participants express views by allocating capital
3. **Resolution** — Outcomes are verified and settled transparently
