# FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Oraculum.

## General

### What is Oraculum?
Oraculum is an AI-powered prediction market platform where users can bet on real-world outcomes. Market prices reflect collective belief about future events.

### How is it different from gambling?
Prediction markets focus on information discovery rather than entertainment. Prices reflect genuine probability estimates, and AI analysis helps filter noise from signal.

### Is Oraculum available worldwide?
Oraculum is accessible through Telegram. Check local regulations regarding prediction markets in your jurisdiction.

## Getting Started

### How do I start?
1. Open Oraculum through Telegram
2. Connect your TON wallet
3. Browse markets
4. Place your first bet

### What wallet do I need?
Oraculum supports TON-compatible wallets including Tonkeeper and TON Wallet.

### Is there a minimum bet?
Minimum bet amounts vary by market. Check individual market details.

## Betting

### How do I win?
Bet on the outcome you believe will happen. If correct, you receive a payout based on the final price.

### What happens if I'm wrong?
You lose your staked amount. Only bet what you can afford to lose.

### Can I sell my position early?
Yes, you can exit positions before market resolution at current market prices.

### How are markets resolved?
Markets are resolved based on verifiable real-world outcomes from trusted sources.

## AI Signals

### Are AI signals guaranteed?
No. AI signals are probabilistic estimates, not guarantees. Always do your own research.

### How accurate is the AI?
Accuracy varies by market type. Historical performance is tracked and displayed.

### Should I follow AI signals blindly?
No. Use AI as one input among many. Combine with your own research and judgment.
